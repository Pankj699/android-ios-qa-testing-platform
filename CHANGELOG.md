## [v1.0 QA] - 2026-10-02 (1.0.0)
- **Type**: INITIAL RELEASE / BASELINE
- **Summary**: Production-ready Web-Based Android QA Testing Platform for Play Asset Delivery (PAD) & On-Demand Resources (ODR).
  - Multi-Device Support: Server ADB, Server Wireless ADB, Browser WebUSB, Browser Wireless QR Pairing.
  - Device Claiming and Multi-User Isolation.
  - Live Mirror with WebCodecs/H.264 video streaming and expanded full-viewport mode.
  - Interactive Screen Recording and Screenshot capture with direct image clipboard copying.
  - Bundletool automated .aab extraction, universal APK generation, and asset pack validation.
  - Comprehensive real-time device telemetry and diagnostics.
